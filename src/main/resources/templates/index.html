About the company

FX Horizon was founded by two engineering students who wanted to understand how currency markets really move. Our platform is built on Java 25 and Spring Boot and talks to two very different services: a classic SOAP web-service of the central bank and a modern REST trading API.

This demo runs on a practice (demo) trading account – no real money is ever used.

Quick start
  1. Check the official MNB rates for a period.
  2. Compare them with the live market price.
  3. Look at the last 10 candles.
  4. Open a position and follow it.
